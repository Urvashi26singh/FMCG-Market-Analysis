# FMCG Market Analysis & Growth Strategy 📊

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 500 FMCG sales transactions using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, profitability, regional performance, category performance, product performance, and monthly sales trends.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to convert the analysis into actionable business insights and growth strategies.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Identify the strongest and weakest regions
- Compare FMCG category performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Track key business KPIs
- Identify opportunities for business growth

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used

- Microsoft Excel

- Business Analysis

## 📊 Dashboard

The interactive dashboard includes:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Top 5 Products
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Interactive Year, Region and Category filters

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### Regional Performance
Identified the highest- and lowest-performing regions based on total sales.

### Category Performance
Compared FMCG categories based on sales, profit and profitability.

### Product Performance
Identified the top 5 products contributing to sales performance.

### Monthly Trends
Analyzed sales patterns over time to identify changes in demand.

### Profitability
Calculated profit margin to understand how efficiently sales are converted into profit.

## 💡 Growth Strategy

Based on the analysis, the following strategies were identified:

- Expand operations and distribution in the strongest region.
- Invest in high-performing FMCG categories.
- Increase marketing and promotion for products with strong profit potential.
- Review pricing and costs for low-margin categories.
- Investigate underperforming regions and improve product availability.
- Use monthly sales trends to support inventory and marketing decisions.
